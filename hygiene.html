Our Process

The 7-Step Hygiene Promise

From the moment a toy returns to the moment it hits your doorstep, it never skips a step.

1
Step 1

Toy Collection

When toys are returned, they are immediately sent to our sanitization facility. Each toy is logged and tracked through the entire process.

2
Step 2

Inspection

Every toy undergoes a 10-point quality and safety inspection. We check for structural integrity, loose parts, sharp edges, and wear. Damaged toys are retired.

3
Step 3

Cleaning

Toys are thoroughly washed using eco-friendly, child-safe cleaning agents. All surfaces, crevices, and textures are carefully cleaned.

4
Step 4

Sanitization

We use medical-grade UV-C sanitization combined with EPA-approved disinfectants. This eliminates 99.9% of germs, bacteria, and viruses.

5
Step 5

Safety Check

A final safety check ensures all toys meet our quality standards. We verify completeness, functionality, and overall condition before approval.

6
Step 6

Sealed Packaging

Approved toys are sealed in tamper-proof, eco-friendly packaging. The seal confirms the toy has passed all hygiene checks and is ready for the next family.

7
Step 7

Delivery

Freshly sanitized toys are packed in our signature ToyLoop box and delivered to your doorstep within 3-5 business days.

Our Safety Standards

Toy Inspection

Each toy is inspected for choking hazards, structural damage, paint quality, and compliance with child safety standards (BIS/CPSIA).

Cleaning Process

Multi-stage cleaning with sonication for hard-to-reach areas, followed by high-pressure rinsing and controlled drying.

Sanitization

UV-C light exposure for 30+ minutes followed by food-grade disinfectant application. Independent lab-tested for effectiveness.

Damaged Toy Handling

Toys that don't meet our standards are permanently retired and recycled responsibly. We never compromise on safety.
