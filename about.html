Why We Exist

From one cluttered playroom to a smarter way to play

ToyLoop began in 2019 with a simple frustration: our homes were overflowing with toys our kids had already outgrown. The average toy is played with for just a few weeks before it's forgotten.

We asked a better question — what if toys could be shared? What if every child could access a constantly refreshed library of premium, perfectly sanitized toys for a fraction of the price?

  • A toy library, reimagined

    Premium toys without the price tag or the waste.

  • Built by parents

    Every decision is shaped by real family routines.

  • Kind to the planet

    Toys keep circulating instead of filling landfills.
